Privacy

Email Builder and the Email Templates Chrome extension, from Exponential Potential. Last updated November 2026.

The Email Builder is a staff tool for Exponential Potential Mathnasium centers. It does not sell or share information with advertisers, and it has no ads or tracking.

Parent report PDFs

PDFs are read inside your own browser. They are not uploaded anywhere, and the email is drafted on your computer.

Signing in to your center

Each center signs in with a center name and password. Those go to the center's template workspace, which is a private Google Sheet run by Exponential Potential. It holds each center's settings (name, address, phone, booking link) and email templates. Templates do not contain student information. Your browser remembers the sign-in on that computer until you sign out.

Report a problem

If you send a problem report, it goes to the Exponential Potential owner and includes your description and technical details. You can choose whether it also includes a screenshot and the PDFs you uploaded. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

Student of the Month

Nominations come from a Google Form run by Exponential Potential. They are loaded into your browser with a separate password and stay there until cleared.

The Gmail side panel extension

The Chrome extension only shows the Email Builder page in Chrome's side panel. It does not read your Gmail or any other web page, and it collects nothing itself. What you type into the template blanks stays in the panel until you copy it.

Outside services

The page loads a few open-source tools (such as a PDF reader) from public content delivery networks, which see your IP address the way any website you visit does. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Questions

Ask your center director, or the owner of Exponential Potential LLC.
